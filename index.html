Sidekick

Sidekick is a personal assistant app built and used only by its creator. It connects to the creator's own Gmail and Google Calendar to help manage email and events.

Privacy Policy

Sidekick does not collect data from any other users. Data from Google services is accessed only to perform actions the creator requests, is stored only in the creator's private database, and is not sold or shared with third parties.
